| Segment | Pain | Promise |

| “Call my dispatcher” with no standard hours | **24/7 operations** plus **24x7 customer service** and **24x7 on-site maintenance** (VAS on live site). |
| No location transparency | **GPS on all units** (stated on live site)—supports shipper need for accountability. |
| Informal fleets, inconsistent safety/ethics | **Ethical business** and **partnership** as explicit company commitments. |
| Little help beyond the truck | **Hire a Helper** VAS for loading/unloading support when the job needs extra hands. |
| Random FB posts as “tracking” | **Honest tracking teaser:** portal coming soon; sales can provide live status today. |
