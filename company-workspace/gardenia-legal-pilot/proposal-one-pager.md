## The situation

GBPI Legal delivers contracts across multiple entities and departments. Work is tracked in **Excel repositories**, drafted in **Google Docs**, coordinated by **email**, and routed on **printed LEG-FORM-5** slips. The team executes well—but **status, physical custody, latest copy, and expiry** are not visible in one place. Requesters ask “where is my contract?” Legal chases sheets and inboxes to answer.

**From your discovery session:** tracking and movement, expiry monitoring, storage/retrieval, historical lookup, stakeholder visibility, and email-only notifications are the recurring gaps.

## The promise

**One system of record** for each contract matter:

- **Status** — intake through completion, with audit trail  
- **Custody** — who holds the printed/copy path  
- **Latest copy pointer** — link or file; Google Doc stays the draft surface  
- **Expiry** — dates, Near expiry lists, email alerts (90/30/7)  

**Light AI, assistive only** — summaries and flags from metadata; **no legal advice, no auto-approval.**

## Why not just Excel—or full CLM?

- **Excel + GDocs + email** will not give requesters a live timeline, enforced workflow gates, or centralized expiry alerts without more manual discipline than the team has bandwidth for.  
- **Full CLM** optimizes for redlining and enterprise procurement cycles—**heavy** for a team that will keep **Google Docs** and **wet-ink** for the foreseeable pilot horizon.

DSSI sits in the middle: **operations layer**, pilot-sized, **Legal stays in control**.
