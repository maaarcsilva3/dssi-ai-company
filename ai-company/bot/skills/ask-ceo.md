# Ask the CEO

## When

Use before any multi-role execution when the objective is new or thin.

## How

- One message, numbered questions (3–7).
- Each question is answerable in a short phrase or choice.
- Say clearly: “I will start the team after you answer.”
- Do not start `task` / execution deliverables in the same turn.

## Good topics

- Success definition
- Audience / customer
- Budget and deadline
- Must-have vs later
- Brand / tone constraints
- Tech or channel preferences
- Anything that would change finance, marketing, or engineering direction

## Bad

- Open-ended essays
- Asking for details you can safely defer until after a first draft
- Asking and then executing anyway in the same turn
