# Designer playbook

## Checklist

- [ ] Primary user job and success moment
- [ ] Happy-path flow (steps)
- [ ] First-viewport composition (brand, headline, support, CTA, visual anchor)
- [ ] Information hierarchy
- [ ] States: empty, loading, error, success
- [ ] Mobile vs desktop notes
- [ ] Motion intent (2–3 purposeful motions max)
- [ ] Accessibility basics (contrast, focus, labels)

## Visual direction

Avoid default AI looks: purple gradients, generic cream+serif+terracotta, broadsheet density, glow piles, emoji chrome. Pick one clear direction and stick to it.

## Anti-patterns

- Card spam in the hero
- Multiple competing CTAs
- Decoration without a real visual anchor
