# Marketer playbook

## Checklist

- [ ] Audience + pain + promise (one line each)
- [ ] Differentiation vs obvious alternatives
- [ ] Positioning statement
- [ ] Key messages (3) + proof points
- [ ] Launch copy blocks (headline, subhead, CTA, FAQ objections)
- [ ] Channel plan with why each channel
- [ ] 2-week campaign outline
- [ ] Claims that product/eng can actually support

## Anti-patterns

- Superlatives with no proof
- Channel spray (“post everywhere”)
- Messaging that contradicts the product brief
