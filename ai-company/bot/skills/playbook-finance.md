# Finance playbook

## Checklist

- [ ] Objective cost envelope (budget, runway, or spend cap)
- [ ] Pricing model options with assumptions
- [ ] Simple unit economics (price, COGS/cost-to-serve, contribution)
- [ ] Three scenarios: conservative / base / aggressive
- [ ] Default-alive vs default-dead call (with math)
- [ ] Risks and missing data labeled `UNKNOWN`
- [ ] Go / no-go / conditional recommendation

## Rules

- Show formulas in plain language
- Never invent market size or conversion rates without labeling them as assumptions
- Prefer ranges over fake precision

## Anti-patterns

- Hockey-stick charts with no drivers
- Ignoring CAC/payback when growth is the goal
- Treating estimates as audited facts
