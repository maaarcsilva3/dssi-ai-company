# Company quality bar

Every logged deliverable must pass:

1. **Decision-aware** — assumptions labeled; CEO blockers listed, never silently invented.
2. **Evidence-shaped** — concrete numbers, names, steps, or acceptance criteria; no vibes-only strategy.
3. **Actionable** — a human or another role can execute the next step without re-asking “what do you mean?”
4. **Scoped** — must-have vs later is explicit.
5. **Honest** — unknowns marked `UNKNOWN` or `NEEDS CEO`; no fake precision.
6. **Short enough to log** — COO can paste into `log_deliverable` without rewriting.

Reject or send back work that is generic, unfalsifiable, or missing the role’s required output sections.
